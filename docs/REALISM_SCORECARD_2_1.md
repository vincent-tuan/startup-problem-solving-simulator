# Scenario 2.1 realism scorecard

This scorecard records evidence, not a marketing claim.

## Targeted dimensions

- Accounting, treasury and capital integrity: debt underwriting, interest, service, covenants, bounded facilities, scarce equity pipeline.
- Customer, contract and revenue lifecycle: finite sourcing inventory, saturation, delayed outreach, invoice states, collection risk, partial payment, disputes, and bad debt.
- Causal fidelity: exposure precedes outcome; consequences are delayed and replayable.
- Information asymmetry: eligibility and exact probabilities are not projected.
- Replay and anti-exploit quality: matched-seed deterministic tests and adversarial command sequences.

## Remaining overall 85/100 gates

An overall 85/100 score still requires distribution calibration and written reviewer sign-off, plus sufficient coverage in workforce, legal/regulatory/insurance/governance, product operations, and correlated external-world regimes.
